#01 About Me

Driven by curiosity, grounded by craft.

Hey, I'm Thivakar. Ever since I was a kid taking apart old toys just to see how the gears meshed, I've had a fascination with what makes things work beneath the surface.

In software, that curiosity naturally pulled me toward backend architecture. While most people are drawn to shiny visual interfaces, I found my thrill in the quiet machinery: designing databases that never lose a byte, untangling race conditions, and creating systems that stay fast and calm even under heavy traffic.

“I believe the best technology is humble and invisible — it does its job so reliably that users take it for granted.”

Currently pursuing my B.Tech at SKCET (graduating 2027). Beyond code, I find balance over a chessboard analyzing tactical endgames, experimenting with mechanical keyboards, and brewing a patient cup of tea.
